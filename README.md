### 🧑‍🔬 About me

- 🏛️ Research fellow at **DIETI – University of Naples Federico II**, member of the **IKNOS** research group
- 🎓 Completed the **Italian National PhD Programme in Artificial Intelligence** (*Agrifood and Environment* area) – thesis defence coming soon
- 🇦🇹 Visiting PhD student at **BOKU University, Vienna** (Institute of Geomatics), March–June 2025
- 📚 M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (*Data Engineering and Artificial Intelligence*), 2023 – 110/110 cum laude, thesis on **knowledge graph population with generative imputation techniques**

### 🧠 Research interests

- 📊 **Data-centric AI** and **data quality**
- 🛰️ **Remote sensing**: satellite image time series, cloud reconstruction, missing-data imputation, change detection
- 🔎 **Information retrieval**: content-based and multimodal retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, robustness of retrieval models
- 🌾 **AI for agrifood and the environment**

### 🚀 Recent work

- 🌍 **EarthJEV** – model, code and paper released on arXiv: [arXiv:2611.02096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02096)
- ☁️ *A Probabilistic Framework for Cloud Reconstruction in Multispectral Satellite Image Time Series* – presented at **KES 2026**, Dublin
- 🌱 Survey on **data-centric AI in agrifood**
